# Plane-Crash-Analysis

## 📌 About the Project
I created a set of connected data dashboards to analyze why airplane accidents happen. This project looks at aviation safety data at three different levels:
1.  **Global Level:** Big trends and accident rates around the world.
2.  **Africa Level:** Common flight safety issues across the African continent.
3.  **Nigeria Level:** A close look at specific aviation history and safety patterns inside Nigeria.


---

## 📊 What Each Dashboard Shows

### 1. Global View
*   Compares plane crash trends across different decades and regions worldwide.
*   It tracks how commercial flight safety has improved globally and shows the most common phases of flight where incidents happen (like takeoff vs. landing).

### 2. Africa View
*   Looks closely at aviation safety across the African continent.
*   It highlights regional challenges, such as older fleets, airport infrastructure gaps, and how air traffic control systems impact safety benchmarks.

### 3. Nigeria View
*   Looked into the history and causes of airplane accidents inside Nigeria.
*   It looks at specific commercial or private incidents, tracking the balance between mechanical issues, weather factors, and human error over time.
